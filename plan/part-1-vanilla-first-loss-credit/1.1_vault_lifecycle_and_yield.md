# Phase 1.1 — Vault Lifecycle and Yield

Part 1 (Vanilla) · Project: first-loss-credit · Spec: merged XLS-65.

## Objective
Create an open-ended Single Asset Vault, deposit lender capital, generate **real** yield by
running one minimal loan cycle, then withdraw above par. Prove that share value rises and
that yield is derived, not stored.

## Independence contract
Inputs: none. Outputs consumed downstream: none. This phase creates its own vault, broker,
and loan purely to move the share price; none of that state is shared.

## Preconditions this phase builds
1. Fund 3 accounts: `owner` (owns the vault **and** the broker), `lender`, `borrower`.
2. Vault asset: for a pure XLS-65 run use **XRP** (simplest). (MPT asset is Part 3.)
3. A vault, a loan broker with cover, and one loan — created inside this phase only to
   produce yield.

## Actors
| Role | Purpose |
|------|---------|
| owner | creates the vault **and** the broker (only `Vault.Owner` may `LoanBrokerSet`), posts cover |
| lender | deposits into the vault, later withdraws with yield |
| borrower | takes the loan, repays with interest (interest is the lender's yield) |

## Transaction steps (exact fields)
1. **VaultCreate** (`tt=65`), `Account=owner` (vault owner; must equal the future broker owner).
   - `Asset`: `{ currency: "XRP" }` (the bare string `"XRP"` fails xrpl@5.1.0 validation).
   - `WithdrawalPolicy`: the first-come policy value (confirm constant in xrpl.js model).
   - Omit `Scale` (forced 0 for XRP), `DomainID`, `tfVaultPrivate` (public vault).
   - Capture `VaultID` and `ShareMPTID` from metadata.
2. **VaultDeposit** (`tt=68`), `Account=lender`, `VaultID`, `Amount` (XRP drops).
   - Read post-state: `AssetsTotal == AssetsAvailable == deposit`, lender share
     `MPToken.MPTAmount == deposit * 10^Scale` (initial-deposit rule; `Scale = 0` for XRP).
3. Build yield: **LoanBrokerSet** (`tt=74`, `Account=owner`) on `VaultID`;
   **LoanBrokerCoverDeposit** (`tt=76`); **LoanSet** (`tt=80`, co-signed, `PaymentTotal=1`,
   short `PaymentInterval`); **LoanPay** (`tt=84`) for the full schedule. (Field detail: see
   the documented procedures in 1.2 and 1.3; reproduce them here — do not read their runtime
   state. Use minimal values; the goal is only to raise `AssetsTotal`.)
4. **VaultWithdraw** (`tt=69`), `Account=lender`, `VaultID`, `Amount` as the **share MPT
   amount** (redeem all shares) or an asset amount.

## Expected results and assertions
- After origination, `AssetsTotal` rises by `InterestDue` while `AssetsAvailable` drops by
  the drawn principal. After full repayment, `AssetsAvailable` returns and includes interest.
- Withdraw pays out **more than the original deposit**: redeemed assets =
  `shares * (AssetsTotal - LossUnrealized) / SharesTotal`, and `LossUnrealized == 0` here.
- `tesSUCCESS` for every step.
- Confirm there is **no `sharePrice`/`accruedYield` field**; compute the rate from
  `vault_info` (`AssetsTotal`, shares `OutstandingAmount`).

## Explorer verification
Record the `VaultCreate`, final `LoanPay`, and `VaultWithdraw` tx hashes with explorer
links showing the lender's net gain.

## Precision and timing rules
- Sole-shareholder exception: with one lender, `LossUnrealized` is waived on redeem.
- Share rounding directions differ: **deposit** rounds the minted share amount **down**
  (XLS-65 §3.1.7.2.1); **withdraw** rounds the share amount to the **nearest** whole number
  (§3.1.7.2.3). Do not state "shares→assets round down" for withdraw.
