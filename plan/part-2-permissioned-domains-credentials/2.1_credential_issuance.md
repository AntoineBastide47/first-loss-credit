# Phase 2.1 — Credential Issuance

Part 2 (Loaded: Permissioned Domains + Credentials) · Spec: XLS-70 Credentials.

## Objective
Issue a credential from an issuer to a subject and have the subject accept it. This is the
membership primitive later used to gate vault depositors.

## Independence contract
Inputs: none. Outputs consumed downstream: none. This phase creates its own issuer and
subject and ends with an accepted credential it does not hand to another phase.

## Key correction (design)
`CredentialCreate` issues **one credential instance to one subject**. It does **not** define
a separate reusable credential *type*; `CredentialType` is a label carried on the instance.

## Preconditions this phase builds
1. Fund `issuer`, `subject`.

## Transaction steps (exact fields)
1. **CredentialCreate**, `Account=issuer`.
   - `Subject`: the `subject` account.
   - `CredentialType`: hex label (e.g. hex of `"KYC_ACCREDITED"`).
   - Optional: `Expiration`, `URI` (hex).
   - Confirm exact field names against the xrpl.js `CredentialCreate` model.
2. **CredentialAccept**, `Account=subject`.
   - `Issuer`: the `issuer` account.
   - `CredentialType`: the same hex label.

## Expected results and assertions
- Both steps `tesSUCCESS`.
- A `Credential` ledger object exists keyed by (`Issuer`, `Subject`, `CredentialType`) and is
  marked accepted after step 2 (unaccepted credentials do not satisfy a permissioned domain).
- Negative control: using the credential before `CredentialAccept` must not grant access in
  phase 2.3.

## Explorer verification
Record `CredentialCreate` and `CredentialAccept` hashes; show the accepted `Credential`
object.

## Friction to capture
- Was the instance-vs-type distinction clear, or did you expect a reusable type object?
- Was the `CredentialType` hex-encoding requirement documented?

## XLS references
