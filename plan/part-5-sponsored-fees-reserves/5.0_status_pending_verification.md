## Verification gate (do before writing 5.1–5.3)

## Tentative phase outline (subject to the gate)
- **5.1 Sponsorship basics** — a sponsor pays the fee and/or reserve for a beneficiary's
  transaction; verify the beneficiary needs no (or less) XRP float.
- **5.2 Sponsored borrower onboarding** — a near-zero-XRP borrower has the loan's owner
  reserve sponsored; verify the `LoanSet` succeeds and the loan object holds.
- **5.3 Sponsored repayment** — the borrower's `LoanPay` fee is paid by the sponsor; verify
  the payment applies and fees route correctly.

## Independence contract (will apply to 5.1-5.3)
Each phase builds its own sponsor, beneficiary, and any vault/broker/loan it needs, and reads
no other phase's state. The shared `lib/` is the only common code.

- Could a sponsor cover a protocol-object reserve (the loan's owner reserve), or only the fee?
